The track

TeamSport Acton's multi-level go-karting track is a twisting 500m circuit in West London — big sweeping turns, speedy electric karts, and one of the fastest courses in the country.

Getting there

Train

Acton Central (London Overground) is closest, about a 10-minute walk. If you're coming via the Underground, Stamford Brook (District), Turnham Green (District / Piccadilly) and East Acton (Central) are around a 20-minute walk — Acton Central is the better call for most people.

By road

From central London, take the A40 Westway west, then the A219 exit towards Harlesden / White City. Follow Wood Lane, South Africa Road, Bloemfontein Road, Uxbridge Road (A4020), Warple Way, then first left onto Allied Way. The venue is on the right.

Parking

Limited free parking on site. If there are no spaces directly outside, parking permits for Rex (further up the street) are available after 5pm on weekdays. Ask the crew at reception for a permit.

The AGP treatment

Smooth Operator

For the most consistent driver who never placed 1st but always finished Top 5.
